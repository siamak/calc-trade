# 📘 Learn Risk Management

_A quick guide on why risk management matters — and how this calculator helps you trade smarter._

---

## 🔒 Why Risk Management?

Risk management is **not about avoiding loss** — it's about **surviving long enough to win**. Whether you're day trading or swing trading, smart risk control separates pros from gamblers.

Here’s why it matters:

- ✅ **Protects Your Capital**  
  Every dollar lost is one less soldier in your trading army. Small, controlled losses keep you in the game.

- 📈 **Improves Consistency**  
  Position sizing aligns your exposure across trades — so one bad setup doesn’t ruin a good week.

- 🧠 **Reduces Emotional Decisions**  
  Predefined risk limits help eliminate panic selling or revenge trading.

- 💡 **Increases Long-Term Survival**  
  Avoiding big drawdowns gives your edge time to play out.

- 🔁 **Enables Compounding**  
  Preserving equity lets your winners stack up over time.

## 📐 Risk Management Formulas

These are the core formulas used in the calculator:

### 1. **Risked Capital**

```
Risked Capital = Account Balance × (Risk % ÷ 100)
```

> Example:  
> $9,000 × 0.25% = **$22.50**

---

### 2. **Position Size**

```
Position Size = Risked Capital ÷ (Stop Loss % ÷ 100)
```

> Example:  
> $22.50 ÷ 2.5% = **$900**

---

### 3. **Margin (with Leverage)**

```
Margin = Position Size ÷ Leverage
```

> Example:  
> $900 ÷ 7 = **$129**

---

### 4. **Take Profit (based on R:R Ratio)**

```
Take Profit $ = Risked Capital × Risk/Reward Ratio
```

> Example:  
> $22.50 × 9 = **$202.50**

---

### 5. **Return on Equity (ROE)**

```
ROE % = (Estimated Profit ÷ Margin) × 100
```

> Example:  
> ($202.50 ÷ $129) × 100 = **157.5%**

## 📌 Example: BTC Futures Trade

Let’s say you have:

- **Account Balance**: $9,000
- **Risk % per Trade**: 0.25%
- **Stop Loss**: 2.5%
- **Leverage**: 7x

### 🔍 Result:

- **Risked Capital**: $22.50
- **Position Size**: $900
- **Margin Used**: $129
- **Take Profit** (with R:R of 1:9): ~$202.50
- **ROE**: +157.5%
- **Balance Impact**: +2.25%

You’re risking only a **tiny 0.25%** of your capital, but if your take-profit hits, you’ll gain nearly **10x that**.  
That’s the power of **asymmetric risk**.

## ✅ Practical Tips

- 📝 **Keep a Trade Journal**  
  Log entries, exits, stop levels, and emotions. Learn from every setup.

- 🔒 **Stick to Fixed Risk %**  
  Risk 0.5%–2% per trade. Never "go all in" emotionally or financially.

- 🎯 **Set Realistic R:R Targets**  
  Aim for at least 1:2 or better — and only take high-conviction setups.

- 💸 **Plan for Fees and Slippage**  
  Factor in exchange fees, funding rates, and spread. They add up!
